Aryan Khatwad's Curated Source Collection

AI-driven job displacement is the topic of this curated collection. Specifically, the purpose of this collection is to explore how the reshaping of the labor market by AI is affecting five stakeholders in this issue: employees, employers, governments, investors, and consumers. I chose this topic because this is a rapidly evolving issue that not only affects my future career plans in the field of computing applications, but society as a whole. The scope of this collection is limited to the five aforementioned stakeholders and their perspectives on the issue, as this will help keep my collection focused on labor displacement by AI as a socio-economic phenomenon, not the technology itself. The sources I have discovered in my research on this topic are organized by stakeholder category (employees, employers, governments, investors, and consumers). This structure works because the issue of AI driven job displacement is not a single-perspective issue, as many parties view it and are affected by it in different manners. In order for readers to use this collection, they can navigate using the navigation bar at the top of each page. Clicking on either the "Employees", "Employers", "Governments", "Investors", or "Consumers" text in the navigation bar will lead to their respective page that contains the sources associated with them and my structured annotations of said sources.
